# Painted tree remains

`dead-fruit-tree-stump-v1.png` is the transparent runtime stump used after a dead neighbor has weathered for four turns. Dead standing trees reuse their exact species and life-stage structure painting with a muted deadwood treatment, preserving their identity and silhouette instead of switching to a procedural shape.

The stump is a low, asymmetric watercolor/gouache specimen with a visible cut surface and broad root flare. It intentionally omits soil, snow, shadow, leaves, and background so every seasonal scene can place it at the ground horizon. The 768×512 runtime copy was downsampled from the preserved 1536×1024 master in `source/`.

The built-in image generator produced the master from `assets/trees/plum-young-tree-v1.png` as the approved bark/root-style reference. The prompt is preserved in `prompts.json`.
